Forensic Financial Research

All reports on this site are currently under re-verification and should not be relied on.

The reports published here were generated by an experimental multi-agent pipeline that reads SEC XBRL filings and produces long-form written analysis. The pipeline is research software. It was not, at any point, a substitute for a human analyst checking the numbers.

In August 2026 the author audited the pipeline's output and found verified factual errors in a related batch of reports.

Because those errors came from the pipeline itself rather than from one bad run, every report on this site has been placed under re-verification — including the ones below, which have not yet been individually re-checked.

What happens next

Two documents will follow and will be published here:

  • A methodology page describing exactly what the pipeline does, what it reads, where it can go wrong, and what a reader would have to verify independently.
  • A public post-mortem of the August 2026 audit: which errors were found, how they were produced, and what changed as a result.

Reports will only come out of re-verification one at a time, once their figures have been checked against the underlying filings.

Why the reports are still up

They remain accessible below for transparency. Taking them down quietly would hide the record of what was published and when. Each one now carries a banner saying the same thing this page says.

These reports are not investment advice and are not a recommendation to buy or sell any security. They are not “institutional grade” research, and any earlier description of them as such was wrong. Nothing here has been reviewed or approved by any regulator. Do your own verification against the primary filings on SEC EDGAR.
