# Forensic Financial Research — on hold

**Every report in this repository is under re-verification. Do not rely on any
number, score or conclusion here until that work is finished.**

[Homepage →](https://thekinghippopotamus.github.io/ForensicAnalysis/)

## What this is

Long-form research notes on US public companies, built from primary sources:
SEC filings and earnings-call transcripts. They are produced by an experimental
automated pipeline, not by a research firm, and they are published for free.

## Why it is on hold

Factual errors were found in a related batch of reports in August 2026. Rather
than leave work of unknown accuracy in front of readers, the whole set was put
into a holding state:

- every report page carries an **UNDER RE-VERIFICATION** banner;
- every report page is marked `noindex,nofollow`, so it is not surfaced by
  search engines;
- no page loads anything from a third party — no external fonts, scripts,
  trackers or analytics.

Pages come back one at a time, and only after their figures have been
re-derived from the underlying filings. Nothing is being quietly edited in
place: corrections will be visible.

## If you read them anyway

Treat every figure as unverified, check it against the filing it cites, and
assume the conclusions may move. Nothing here is investment advice, a
recommendation, or an offer of any kind. It is information and education only.
